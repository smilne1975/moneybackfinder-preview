About MoneyBack Finder

MoneyBack Finder is a simple, independent directory of class-action settlements, regulator and government redress schemes, and official unclaimed-money tools in Australia and the United Kingdom.

Our rules

  • Official links only. Buttons go to the court, regulator, government agency, or the law firm or administrator running the case. We never link to aggregators or claims-management lead forms.
  • Honest status. Each listing says whether claims are open, registration is open, money is awaiting distribution, or payment is automatic. Unknown details are shown as unknown.
  • Sourced and dated. Each page lists the official pages checked and when.
  • Free. We don't file claims, take a cut or collect personal details.

Disclaimer

General information only, not legal or financial advice. MoneyBack Finder is not a law firm, court, settlement administrator, regulator or government agency. A listing doesn't mean you qualify, and isn't a recommendation to join a class action. Joining may affect your rights (for example, you may be bound by a settlement). Read the official notice, and get advice from the lawyers running the case or your own adviser if unsure.

Privacy

This prototype has no accounts, no tracking cookies and no forms that collect data.
