FAQ

Who is eligible?

UK consumers in the class. The official site says you can register for a payment whether or not you ever held a Mastercard. Check the site for the exact class definition when claims open. The official page has the full definition, so check it before acting.

What do I need to do now?

Usually nothing new can be lodged. If you already registered, keep your contact details up to date and watch for official emails (beware of scams). If a claims window is still to open, sign up on the official site to be told.

Do I need proof?

No proof needed. The site says claims will use a simple online form.

How much could I get?

Official site says individuals could get between £45 and £70 each. Any amounts are estimates until final.

Does it cost anything?

No. Claiming or registering on the official site is free. Lawyers' fees in class actions are usually paid out of any settlement with court approval. You don't need a claims management company.
