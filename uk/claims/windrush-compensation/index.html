FAQ

Who is eligible?

You suffered losses because you could not show your right to live in the UK, and you (or your parents or grandparents) came from a Commonwealth country before 1973, or you came from any country before 31 December 1988 and are now settled. Some close family members and estates can also apply. The official page has the full definition, so check it before acting.

What do I need to do now?

Claim on the official site linked on this page.

Do I need proof?

Proof required. Evidence of losses (employment, housing, health, etc.).

How much could I get?

The official source doesn't state an individual amount. Any amounts are estimates until final.

Does it cost anything?

No. Claiming or registering on the official site is free. Lawyers' fees in class actions are usually paid out of any settlement with court approval. You don't need a claims management company.
