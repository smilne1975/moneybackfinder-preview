FAQ

Who is eligible?

You (or your business) bought or financed a new car or van in the UK for your own use between 18 October 2006 and 6 September 2015 (some brands excluded). You can then claim for eligible new vehicles bought or leased up to 31 December 2019. The official page has the full definition, so check it before acting.

What do I need to do now?

Usually nothing new can be lodged. If you already registered, keep your contact details up to date and watch for official emails (beware of scams). If a claims window is still to open, sign up on the official site to be told.

Do I need proof?

Not stated.

How much could I get?

£25 for the first eligible vehicle, £5 each for the 2nd to 6th, £2.50 each after that. First come, first served until the fund runs out. Any amounts are estimates until final.

Does it cost anything?

No. Claiming or registering on the official site is free. Lawyers' fees in class actions are usually paid out of any settlement with court approval. You don't need a claims management company.
