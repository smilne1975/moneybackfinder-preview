Unclaimed money in the UK: free official tools

These are free government or official services. None of them needs a paid 'finder'.

Tip: Search under current and former names and addresses. Check old workplace pensions, Child Trust Funds (born 2002–2011) and Premium Bonds prizes.

Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS)

Run by: FSCS

Compensation when an authorised financial firm (bank, adviser, insurer, pension provider) has failed.

How: Check whether the firm has failed and claim directly with the FSCS. It's free; you don't need a claims firm.

Financial Ombudsman Service

Run by: Financial Ombudsman Service

Free, independent decisions on complaints against financial firms (e.g. car finance, BSPS) after the firm has replied.

How: Complain to the firm first. If you're unhappy with its final response, take it to the Ombudsman (the firm's reply explains time limits).

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. My Lost Account, NS&I, HMRC, the Pension Tracing Service, the FSCS and the Financial Ombudsman are all free. You don't need a claims company or a tax-refund agent.

I had an old bank or building society account. What now?

Use My Lost Account. One search covers banks, building societies and NS&I.

How do I find an old pension?

Use the Pension Tracing Service on GOV.UK to get the scheme's contact details, then ask the scheme directly.

How do I avoid scams?

HMRC and NS&I won't text or email asking for bank details to 'release' a refund. Go to GOV.UK or the official site directly.
