Unclaimed money in Australia: free official searches

These are free government or official services. None of them needs a paid 'finder'.

Tip: Search every state or territory you've lived in, under current and former names, and check your super in myGov.

Moneysmart unclaimed money search (ASIC)

Run by: ASIC Moneysmart

Searches money held by ASIC (bank accounts, shares, life insurance) and links to other searches.

How: Search your name on Moneysmart's 'Find unclaimed money'. Then also check state registers and lost super.

ATO lost and unclaimed super (via myGov)

Run by: Australian Taxation Office

Lost super accounts and ATO-held super.

How: Sign in to myGov, link the ATO, choose Super, then Fund details. Lost accounts show 'Contact fund'; ATO-held super can be transferred or paid out. Phone 13 28 65 if you can't use myGov.

FAQ

Is it free to search and claim?

Yes. Moneysmart, the ATO and every state register are free. You never need to pay a 'money finder'.

Which registers should I search?

Moneysmart (ASIC-held money), your super via myGov, and the register in every state or territory you've lived in. Rental bonds have their own process in each state, and old unclaimed bonds can end up on the state register.

What about lost super?

Log in to myGov, link the ATO and open Super › Fund details. You can usually consolidate lost or ATO-held super into your current fund online.

How do I avoid scams?

Government agencies don't charge a fee to release your money. Type official addresses yourself and don't trust unexpected texts or calls.
