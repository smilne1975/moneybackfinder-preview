FAQ

Who is eligible?

Group members as defined in the MLC MySuper class action (see the official page and Settlement Distribution Scheme). The official page has the full definition, so check it before acting.

What do I need to do now?

Usually nothing. Eligible people are paid or contacted automatically. Keep your contact details up to date with the fund or firm.

Do I need proof?

No proof needed. Distributed by the MLC Super Fund trustee (NULIS) under the Court-approved scheme.

How much could I get?

The official source doesn't state an individual amount. Any amounts are estimates until final.

Does it cost anything?

No. Claiming or registering on the official site is free. Lawyers' fees in class actions are usually paid out of any settlement with court approval. You don't need a claims management company.
